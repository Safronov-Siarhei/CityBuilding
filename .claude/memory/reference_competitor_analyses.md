`D:\K&C\kingdoms_and_castles_architecture.md` and `D:\K&C\farthest_frontier_architecture.md`
(written 2026-08-28/29, in Russian, same structure both). Each has: layered architecture
reconstruction, subsystem-by-subsystem behaviour, full building catalogue with costs/workers,
concrete balance figures, critic scores AND the recurring criticisms, and a "what to borrow"
section. Same files are attached in the "Kingdom and Castles" project on claude.ai.

Supersedes the older, thinner [[kc_reference_analysis]] for K&C.

**Why they matter here:** the 2026-08-23 audit ([[project_balance_audit]]) found holes that both
games have already solved — FF splits Desirability (local, gates the house upgrade) from Happiness
(global, 11 factors); FF's research runs on its OWN currency (knowledge points from an Academy),
not on coins; FF's raider camps pay 12 months of peace for clearing them, which is a job for an
army beyond the finishing blow; K&C puts a per-year GOLD UPKEEP on towers, which is exactly what
our free 25-dps tower lacks; FF's Monuments are a three-branch endgame with a win condition.

**What NOT to borrow:** FF's physically carried resources (porters/wagons as a bottleneck) —
our frame budget is already spent on walking citizens, see [[project_performance_baseline]].

**Both games' reviews name the same failure mode**: the mid/late game sags once the economy is
stable. K&C is additionally criticised for having no overlays/diagnostics — our own first
playtest died of exactly that (a Sawmill with no trees in reach, invisible).
