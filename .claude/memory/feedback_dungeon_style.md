A dungeon map starts from the MAIN MAP's style: its geometry and forms (map5.py's distance-field relief, smooth colour zones, the terrain decor kit). Reference: Warcraft -- saturated, bright, greenery welcome underground.
**Why:** v1 of Dungeon 1 (grid of boxes + crags, grey floor) was rejected 2026-10-31 as «cubic, monotonous, too simple».
**How to apply:** no box/grid walls or chequered floors; rock = relief with moss at the foot and a dark craggy top; floors = grass/moss/path zones; add colour landmarks (glowing giant mushrooms, crystals). Related: [[project-dungeon-entrance]], [[feedback-cheap-means-detail]].

2026-10-31: NO right angles / square rooms -- organic shapes (bowed passages, blob halls, smooth unions); fewer passages but more detail (themed niches); no crystals on the ground -- the reward crystal floats in the air.
