The rules are in Design/features/combat-core.md (the top note).

Draft v3 was sent on 2026-11-02 (the 2x2 tower made SQUARE, no round towers, so walls can join it). Before that, v2 came after the user's feedback:
- dark stone, no white bands;
- more merlons;
- doorways only toward walls that actually join the tower.

The user approved the heights and the rule "lvl1-2 tower is no way up".

**Why:** the user wants walls to change the fight, not just gain health.
Models approved (v4 + arched windows). The combat rules are confirmed: melee orcs can't reach fighters on the wall, throwers can; +2 cells of range; a fighter falls and dies when his wall cell breaks. All of it is BUILT in 704b1803; details are on the combat-core card.

**How to apply:** expect playtest feedback (climb look, the auto-seek radius, pathing to a stair foot). A general test run is still pending.
