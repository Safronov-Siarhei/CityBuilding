**The user's idea (2026-10-20):** the player starts on a base with a tower at every entrance; orcs attack constantly and grow stronger over time; the player upgrades the base, builds, hires and upgrades troops, and plays as the King (hero) who can help repel attacks. The point is to last as long as possible; that earns rating points and prizes; daily/weekly/monthly leaderboards; top players get in-game content, currency or other rewards.

**My view (told the user):** the most realistic and best-fitting of the new modes — single-player, no netcode, reuses the city builder, orc waves/kinds/chief, towers, army, research; the King hero would be shared with the lane mode ([[project_lane_mode]]). Leaderboards need only a small backend (Google Play Games / Unity Leaderboards / PlayFab). Risks to design for: cheating on prize leaderboards (client-reported scores -> server checks, fixed seeds, plausibility limits; prizes better cosmetic/soft currency), fairness (same map + same wave seed per period), run length on mobile (ramp so typical runs are 15-30 min), save-scumming (ranked runs without free saves), score formula (reward skill, not grinding).

**How to apply:** treat as under discussion until the user decides; open questions are listed in the conversation reply of 2026-10-20 (economy inside a run, the map, scoring, prize types, leaderboard backend).
