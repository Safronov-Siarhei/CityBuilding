## The user's brief
Do NOT copy Kingdoms & Castles: take its SIMPLICITY and JUICY saturated colour. Mobile first. Chunky
blocks with small chamfers, ribbed roofs of geometry, dark timber on cream walls, flat colour + a soft
detail atlas (2026-10-14, "variant 3"; a desaturated palette was REJECTED). Everything must read as ONE
set. Models go to the user for approval as renders BEFORE they enter the game; pilot-of-four
(Hovel, Cottage, Warehouse, Sawmill) → approval → the rest.

## Files and tools
- Placeholder boxes: `D:\blender projects\Buildings3D\Buildings-Size1.blend` — READ-ONLY for Claude.
  Claude writes `Buildings-Size1-Models.blend` next to it.
- Blender 5.2.2: `D:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Blender\blender.exe --background <Models.blend> --python <script>`;
  scripts print `MODEL … tris=` and `FITS` / `DOES NOT FIT`.
- `Tools/blender/`: `kit.py` (palette 40 cells — FULL; primitives; `kit.run`, `kit.borrow`),
  `details.py` (THE STANDARD sizes + shared details), `detail.py` (detail atlas, `OVERRIDE`),
  one script per model `<id>1.py`, `<id>1_lvl2..5.py` (lvl4/5 GENERATED by `gen_lvl4.py`/`gen_lvl5.py` —
  edit the tables), `orcs.py` + `orc*.py`, `trees1.py`, `stones1.py`, `road1.py`, map generators,
  `render_preview.py`, `render_lineup.py`, `render_icons.py`, `export_fbx.py`, `export_map.py`.
- Standard (details.py): 1 unit = 1 m = 1 cell; storey 0.56; door 0.26×0.50; window 0.20×0.24; roof
  45°; citizen 0.45 m. Footprint may NOT exceed the box; height may. Roof colour by category: City
  terracotta, Town Hall/Defence blue, Storage red, Entertainment violet, Production slate, Food straw.
- Levels: L2 «развитие того же здания»; L3 crowned (finials, turrets, gold); L4 glazed diamond roofs +
  gilded crest; L5 golden roofs (Food red-orange — gold vanishes on straw). L(N) borrows L(N−1): rerun
  higher levels after editing a lower one.
- Export: `export_fbx.py -- D:/Fork/CityBuilding [Name ...]` → `Models/Buildings|Terrain|Orcs`; pass
  names to avoid exporting everything. Origin = plot centre, Blender (x,y,z) = Unity (x,z,y).
  Material `Buildings` remapped at import (`Editor/ModelImportDefaults.cs`) onto
  `Models/Palette/BuildingsPalette.mat` (shader `Shaders/BuildingDetail.shader`) — updated IN PLACE,
  never deleted (remaps are by GUID).
- The Invasion round orc camps: `orcringcamp1.py` builds OrcRingWall (one 3.9 m ring section, lean:
  401 tris), OrcRingWall-Ruin, OrcRingGate (open arch); everything else is the camps' existing models.
- The Invasion jungle: `wild.py` builds WildWolf/WildBear (four legs LegFL/FR/BL/BR), WildTroll/WildGiant
  (LegL/R, ArmStrike, ArmOff), WildShrine; `export_fbx` sends `Wild*` to `Models/Wild`; `render_lineup.py`
  now shows a jointed figure's parts too.
- Figures: `heroking.py` builds the King JOINTED (legs, arms, head as child parts with origins at the
  joints); `export_fbx` sends `Hero*` with its parts to `Models/Heroes`; animation is code
  (`Combat/FigureAnimator.cs`), no rigs. `soldiers.py` builds the army the same way (`Soldier*` →
  `Models/Soldiers`, parts excluded like Hero's); `render_figures.py -- <out> Name...` renders jointed
  figures side by side at one scale. `orevein1.py` (4 vein models); gates remodelled 1×1 / 1×3 in
  all levels (`gate1*.py`, `fortifiedgate1*.py`).
- Icons: `render_icons.py -- <out> 192` → `Assets/_Project/UI/Icons` (re-render after model changes).
- App icon: placeholder village-with-windmill `UI/AppIcon.png`; a final icon is open.

## Traps
- The palette image must be RECREATED every run; palette cells are append-only.
- The detail atlas picks its pattern BY COLOUR (rust roof → town tiles; orc models override).
- `Orchard*` starts with `Orc` → export once routed it to Models/Orcs (fixed). `glob("*_lvl4.py")`
  matched the generator itself — glob `*1_lvl4.py`.
- A roof over the defining prop hides it from the 50° camera — put props in the open yard.
- Never `cat > file` without a heredoc; a scratchpad `inspect.py` shadows the stdlib (`python -I`).
- Delete `Tools/blender/__pycache__/`; never commit it.
